# phone.deleteGroupCallMessages

Delete specific messages from the [in-call message overlay »](/api/group-calls.md#in-call-messages) of a video chat/livestream or live story, including in RTMP mode.

Non-admin participants may delete messages they sent; admins may delete any message.
