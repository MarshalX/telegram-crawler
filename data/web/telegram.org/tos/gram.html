Gram Wallet is non-custodial. Your private keys and recovery phrase are generated and stored on your device. We do not receive them, cannot see them, and cannot use them. Neither we nor Telegram can freeze, seize, move, recover or reverse your assets.

This also means that if you lose your recovery phrase, you lose access to your wallet. Nobody can restore it for you. Keep your recovery phrase secure, private and backed up. Storing your recovery phrase as a digital file – in a screenshot, a note, or a message, or cloud storage – increases the risk that it can be stolen. We are not responsible for any loss caused by someone gaining access to your device, cloud storage, or your recovery phrase.

As an additional recovery measure, a distributed encrypted backup may be generated for your Gram Wallet that may be used as an alternative means of restoring access. However, the recovery phrase is the only guaranteed means of recovering your Gram Wallet. Neither Gram Wallet Provider nor Telegram can access your encrypted backup. If you prefer to not have an encrypted backup for your wallet, you may disable it via your wallet's settings inside the Telegram application.

Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from RAZOR, “Gram Wallet support”, or Telegram. We will never message you first, never ask for your recovery phrase or private keys, and never ask you to send a payment to verify or unlock your wallet – anyone who does is a scammer.

6. Account and transaction visibility

By accessing your Gram Wallet inside Telegram, your Telegram account becomes linked to the specific TON address of the wallet. Anyone who visits your Telegram profile may be able to see your associated Gram Wallet address, as well as the total balance contained in your Gram Wallet.

The Telegram interface does not show other users your Gram Wallet transaction history, but every transaction you make with Gram Wallet is recorded on the TON blockchain, and therefore publicly available. Anyone who searches the TON address of your Gram Wallet on a blockchain explorer platform will be able to see every transaction your wallet participates in, including the sender, recipient, assets and amounts transferred.

While Telegram profiles linked to Gram Wallets are not stored in the blockchain, if a user chooses to link unique domains or other assets to their Gram Wallet that correlate to their Telegram profile, those details will become permanently recorded in the blockchain and will be accessible through blockchain explorer platforms.
