### Parameters

| **profiles\_enabled** | [flags](/mtproto/TL-combinators.md#conditional-fields).1?[true](/constructor/true.md) | If set, messages from channel admins will link to their profiles, just like for group messages: can only be set if the `signatures_enabled` flag is set. |
