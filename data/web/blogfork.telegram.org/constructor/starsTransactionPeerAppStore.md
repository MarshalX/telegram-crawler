# starsTransactionPeerAppStore

Describes a [Telegram Star](/api/stars.md) transaction with the App Store, used when purchasing Telegram Stars through the App Store.

```tl
starsTransactionPeerAppStore#b457b375 = StarsTransactionPeer;
```

### Parameters

This constructor does not require any parameters.

### Type
[StarsTransactionPeer](/type/StarsTransactionPeer.md)

### Related pages

#### [Telegram Stars](/api/stars.md)
Telegram Stars are virtual items that allow users to purchase digital goods and services from bots and mini apps inside the Telegram ecosystem, send gifts to content creators on the Telegram platform, and more.
