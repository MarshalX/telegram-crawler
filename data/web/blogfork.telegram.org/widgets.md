# Telegram Widgets

Telegram widgets offer a quick way to add a [**sharing button**](/widgets/share.md) to your pages, [**embed posts**](/widgets/posts.md) from public [channels](https://telegram.org/tour/channels) or [groups](https://telegram.org/tour/groups). Widgets can also help your users [**log in with Telegram**](/widgets/login.md) or view [**Telegram Discussions**](/widgets/discussion.md) on your website.
